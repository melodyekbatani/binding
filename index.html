Notes on Typography

Colonophon

In understanding typography as a methodology for desision making, I've begun to realize how design systems can clarify ideas, reveal limitations or quietly flatten design into a singular "default" state. This collection brings together three distinct viewpoints on the role of typography, and the larger design context for which it inhabits. Composed of three readings: Anthony Froshaugh's Typography Is a Grid, Lorraine Wild's Castles Made of Sand, and Silvia Sfligiotti's This Is Auto-Tune Typography, the project traces typography across moments of structured principle, instability, and automation. Through which it further examines how the systems we build organize meaning and redistribute authorship between designer and tool. The distillation of this is reflected in the exploration of type as a guiding principle, realized through the web's grain.

To reference the specificity of each text, I created two key images that reflect the relationship between the three texts. I explore the idea of excavating the grid, the designer's role in manipulating it into various contextual points, and how when mass applied, typography becomes a mog-pog of similar stylistic templates. I visualized this as a shell being extracted, broken down by hands moving and manipulating it, and the amalgamation of the points into a version of typography that starts to become monotone.

Throughout this process, I introduced key systems for both creating responsive design and one that translate throughout each of the readings. I began by critically analyzing the reading and picking key elements we wanted to integrate in the design of the webpage. This process included pulling key quotes and developing a system for sub-headers to make the design easier to follow. I implemented this into the semantic structure, breaking down each reading as distinct parts of a whole. Once I established a strong hierarchy, I began experimenting with typography and applied various sizing and vertical spacing to break from the web's default design while optimizing it for readability across various breakpoints. I began styling through mobile-first design and found ways to optimize the design throughout various breakpoints.

Building Notes on Typography was a foundational step in understanding how typography and systems shaped code through the web's grain. Working on this project shaped my ability to think structurally, work with constraints and shape a conceptual framework through design. My process is a direct reflection of the three readings, showing me that typography is a framework for expression, and that setting up rules can lead to interesting outcomes!
